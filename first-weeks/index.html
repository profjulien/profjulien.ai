Three things that bite early

  1. Listen first, and the team wonders whether anything will change. Decide early, and they wonder whether you listened.
  2. Each person on the team needs something different from you, and you have a few one-to-ones to work out what.
  3. Some people will have to work differently, including those who were there long before you. You are the one who tells them.

What happens in the 30 minutes
