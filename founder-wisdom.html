The many worlds of enough

no link yet

Success

On the interplay between ambition, greed, settling for enough and self awareness. Settling for enough is elusive because when we reach a certain enough, we are no longer the person that once desired it. Ambition to become a better person has pushed us to evolve our identity and moved the goal post of enough upwards. Byproducts we also get from that process are bumps in salary, status, power or reputation. Once these byproducts become our primary desires, ambition turns to greed and it is time for course correction towards a lower enough through increased self awareness.

Did Elon Musk get lucky? Skill, luck and imitation

Luca Dellanna  ·  essay

Skill vs. luck

The skill-luck tradeoff for outcomes: if you don't take extreme risks, even if you're the most skilled person, you will be outperformed by someone who did. And if you take extreme risks, you will have worse average outcomes than if you didn't. Competing for first place often means using strategies that reduce your average outcome.
